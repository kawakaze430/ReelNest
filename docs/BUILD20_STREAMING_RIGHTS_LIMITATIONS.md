# ReelNest — media licensing, DRM and geoblocking limits

**Not legal advice.** Obtain expert review and valid streaming licences in every intended market.

- Metadata and trial samples do not confer rights to redistribute film footage, artwork, subtitle tracks or dubbed audio.
- Rights records must specify permitted territory, start/end dates, platform (web/mobile/TV), SVOD/AVOD/TVOD tier, language/version, device restrictions and rights holder approval. Existing rights rows do not yet cover all these dimensions; treat worldwide licensing as blocked.
- A globally available domain/CDN does not imply globally available movie rights. Titles must be filtered by an independently verified location signal at session creation, renewal and distribution. VPN bypass prevention is inherently imperfect.
- Existing CloudFront signed cookies are access-control tools, not copy protection/DRM. Previously issued cookies may remain usable until expiration despite DB-side unpublishing. Require short lifetimes, emergency key/asset invalidation procedure and independent acceptance tests.
- Widevine/FairPlay/PlayReady require licensed provider credentials, encrypted packaging, playback SDK/device validation and contracts. None is production approved.
- Offline downloads must use legally permitted encryption and licence expiry; do not expose ordinary decrypted MP4 downloads.
- Upon rights complaint, staff can review and unpublish; public submission does not immediately revoke a film. Disputed rights documents and complaints must be secured and reviewed with legal counsel.
- Age-rating, privacy, payments and regional consumer rules require local legal review; no code feature substitutes for it.
